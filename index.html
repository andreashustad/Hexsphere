The idea

The board is a globe made of hexagons — plus exactly twelve pentagons, which is the only way hexagons can close into a sphere. There are no edges and no corners to hide behind, so every cell has five or six neighbours and the whole board is one continuous field.

Controls

  • Drag to spin the globe. It keeps coasting when you let go.
  • Double tap a covered cell to open it.
  • Tap a covered cell to flag a mine.
  • Tap a number whose flags are all placed to open the rest around it.
  • Pinch or use the zoom buttons to get closer.
  • Hint points at a cell you can already prove, and says why.

Keyboard

  • Arrows spin, +/- zoom.
  • Space opens the centre cell, F flags it, C auto-opens around it.
  • H hint, N new game.

Guaranteed solvable

With this on, each board is generated and then checked by a solver that only makes provable deductions. If the board would ever force a guess, it is rebuilt until it does not. Losing is always your reading of the board, never bad luck.

Hexsphere is a from-scratch rebuild of a globe minesweeper that disappeared from the Play Store. No ads, no tracking, no network — it runs entirely offline once loaded.
